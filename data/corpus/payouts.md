# Payouts

## Schedule
Standard payouts run daily on a T+2 schedule: funds from a payment are paid out two business days after the payment succeeds.

## Minimum payout
The minimum payout amount is $1.00. Balances below the minimum roll over to the next payout.

## New accounts
Payouts for newly created accounts are held for the first 7 days while Meridian completes risk review.

## Instant payouts
Instant payouts deliver funds to an eligible debit card within minutes and cost 1.5% of the amount.

## Failed payouts
If a payout fails because of an invalid bank account, the funds return to your Meridian balance and a `payout.failed` event is sent.
