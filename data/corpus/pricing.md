# Pricing

## Card payments
Card payments cost 2.9% of the transaction amount plus a fixed fee of $0.30.

## ACH debits
ACH debit payments cost 0.8% of the transaction amount, capped at $5.00 per transaction.

## Other fees
- Disputes: a flat $15.00 fee per dispute, refunded if the dispute is resolved in the merchant's favor.
- Instant payouts: 1.5% of the payout amount.
- Standard payouts and refunds have no additional fee.

There are no monthly fees or setup fees on the standard plan.
